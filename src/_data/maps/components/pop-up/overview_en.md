## Usage

Shows additional information about a map marker in a pop-up.

## Rules

- Keep the pop-up visible within the map viewport. Its position may change to the left, below or to the right when the map is moved.
- On small screens, show the content in a box at the bottom of the screen, like a sidebar or card. The box has no fixed height.
- Provide a close button in the upper-right corner.
- If a marker has several pieces of information, show them vertically. Increase the pop-up height as needed or use pagination.

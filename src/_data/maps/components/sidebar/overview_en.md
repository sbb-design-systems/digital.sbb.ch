## Usage

Shows additional information about a map marker in a sidebar.

## Rules

- On desktop, show the sidebar along the left edge at the full height of the map. On small screens, show the content in a box at the bottom of the screen, like a pop-up or card. The box has no fixed height.
- Provide a close button in the upper-right corner.
- When the user selects another marker, keep the sidebar open and replace its content.

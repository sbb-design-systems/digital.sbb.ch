## Usage

Shows walking and public transport routes on the map.

## Rules

- Show public transport routes in black and white, without distinguishing between modes of transport.
- Base the public transport route design on the timetable's route diagram. Leave a gap between the route line and each stop before a transfer. Selecting or hovering over a route shows intermediate stops.
- Show route sections affected by an incident as red dashed lines.
- Show walking routes in blue on a neutral background. At close zoom levels, include arrowheads to indicate the direction of travel. At wider zoom levels, use a solid blue line.
- If route information is unavailable for a section, show the line as a curve.
- Position labels for starting points, destinations and transfer points above, to the right, below or to the left of the point.
- Selecting or hovering over a route section can show additional information about public transport or walking in a pop-up.

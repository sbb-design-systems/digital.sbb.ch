Start page of an application, divided into tabs: key figures with a period filter, personal favourites as quick access and open tasks with a status filter.

**Strengths**
- Key information at a glance, without switching to the lists.
- Tabs keep the page clear, even when several areas come together.
- The badge on the tasks tab shows whether something needs to be done.

**Weaknesses**
- Shows only a selection; complete data requires the lists.
- Content in tabs is hidden and easier to overlook.

**When to use**
As the entry point to applications where users regularly check key figures or work through tasks. For working with all items, the dashboard links to the <sbb-link href="/en/design-system/lyne/templates/list-table/">table list</sbb-link> or the <sbb-link href="/en/design-system/lyne/templates/list-card/">card list</sbb-link>.

Shows a list of items as a table, with filters, sorting via the column headers and actions per row.

**Strengths**
- Compact: many items at a glance.
- Values are easy to compare in columns.

**Weaknesses**
- Requires horizontal scrolling on small screens.

**When to use**
For data-heavy applications used mainly on desktop. For mobile use, the <sbb-link href="/en/design-system/lyne/templates/list-card/">card list</sbb-link> is the better choice.

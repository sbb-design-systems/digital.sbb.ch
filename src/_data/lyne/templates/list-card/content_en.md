Shows a list of items as cards, with filters, sorting and actions per card.

**Strengths**
- Responsive: content rearranges depending on the screen width, without horizontal scrolling.
- Long titles may wrap.

**Weaknesses**
- Fewer items at a glance.
- Values are harder to compare.

**When to use**
For applications that are also used on smartphones. If many items need to be visible and comparable, the <sbb-link href="/en/design-system/lyne/templates/list-table/">table list</sbb-link> is the better choice.

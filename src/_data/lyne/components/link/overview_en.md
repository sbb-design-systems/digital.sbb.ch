
## What does the component do?
A link is an interactive text element that leads to another page or resource. It is available as a block link, as an inline link and as a variant for buttons.

## When should the component be used?
* To guide users to further content, pages or external resources.
* To refer to related information or documents.

## Rules.
* Link texts are descriptive and meaningful. They make the target content understandable.
* A link navigates. For actions, use a [button](/en/design-system/lyne/components/button/).
* Links to external resources open in a new tab.


## What does the component do?
A lead container forms the entry of a product page. It shows a large lead image and below it the introductory content such as breadcrumb, title and lead text. It is intended for content pages only, not for business apps.

## When should the component be used?
* As the entry of product or topic pages that are introduced with an image.
* To combine the title and introduction of a page with an image in a consistent way.

## Rules.
* Use at most one lead container per page, placed after the header.
* The content follows this order: alerts, breadcrumb, back link, title, lead text, notifications.
* Use the predefined Lyne classes for the spacing between the content elements.
* The lead image needs an alternative text. If it is purely decorative, it is hidden from screen readers.


## What does the component do?
A lead container forms the entry of a product page. It shows a large lead image and below it the introductory content such as [breadcrumb](/en/design-system/lyne/components/breadcrumb/), title and lead text. It is intended for content pages only, not for business apps.

## When should the component be used?
* As the entry of product or topic pages that are introduced with an image.
* To combine the title and introduction of a page with an image in a consistent way.

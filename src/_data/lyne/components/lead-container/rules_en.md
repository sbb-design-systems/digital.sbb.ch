## Rules.
* Use at most one lead container per page, placed after the [header](/en/design-system/lyne/components/header/).
* The content follows this order: [alerts](/en/design-system/lyne/components/alert/), breadcrumb, back link, title, lead text, [notifications](/en/design-system/lyne/components/notification/).
* Use the predefined Lyne classes for the spacing between the content elements.
* The lead image needs an alternative text. If it is purely decorative, it is hidden from screen readers.

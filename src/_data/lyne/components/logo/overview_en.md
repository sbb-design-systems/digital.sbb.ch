
## What does the component do?
The component displays the SBB logo with the correct aspect ratio and protective room.

## When should the component be used?
* To show the SBB brand on a page or in an application.

## Rules.
* The logo is placed in the [header](/en/design-system/lyne/components/header/). Its presentation and position are predefined there.
* The logo must neither be changed nor hidden.
* The logo is displayed only once per page.
* The protective room around the logo must stay free. The ideal protective room is the default; use minimal or none only in exceptional cases.
* On dark backgrounds, use the negative variant.

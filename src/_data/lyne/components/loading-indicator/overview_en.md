
## What does the component do?
A loading indicator shows that an action or process is running in the background and users have to wait for this process to finish. It is intended for use as an overlay and is available in red, smoke and white.

## When should the component be used?
* To inform users that an action or process is running in the background.
* To cover waiting times.
* To give feedback that the application is responding to user input.
* When a whole area or the whole page is overlaid while loading. For use within content, the loading indicator circle is suitable.

## Rules.
* Use the indicator only for actions lasting at least about 1 second—shorter processes tend to be confusing due to the flashing. A brief minimum display duration (guideline: about 500 ms) may be useful afterward, but it is not required—depending on what happens next.
* Make sure that the loading indicator does not interfere with the layout of the page or application.
* If possible, offer a progress indicator to give users an idea of the remaining waiting time, especially for long waiting times  (approx. 10 seconds or more).

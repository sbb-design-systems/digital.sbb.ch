## What does the component do?
The teaser hero shows a large image with a red panel containing a short text and a link. It should catch the eye and have an emotional effect.

## When should the component be used?
* To promote a topic, product or service prominently with a strong image.
* As an entry point to further content or pages.

## Rules.
* The image supports the message and is high-quality and emotional.
* The text in the panel is short and inviting. The link describes its target.
* Do not slot block elements (e.g. `div`) into the panel, so that the semantics remain correct for screen readers.
* Do not stack several teaser heroes directly or mix them with other teaser types.

## Rules.
* The sidebar is used for fullscreen applications. Only one sidebar per side (left/right) is allowed.
* In "side" mode, the sidebar sits next to the content. In "over" mode, a backdrop covers the content. With limited space, "side" automatically switches to "over".
* At most one sidebar in "over" mode may be open at a time.
* Always provide a button to open and close the sidebar, ideally in the [header](/en/design-system/lyne/components/header/). Otherwise users cannot open it when space is limited.
* Give the sidebar a title and, if needed, a close button.
* If the sidebar is combined with the [icon sidebar](/en/design-system/lyne/components/icon-sidebar/), one of them uses the "milk" colour variant. This visually differentiates the two components.

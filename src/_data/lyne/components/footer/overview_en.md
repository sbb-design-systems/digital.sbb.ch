
## What does the component do?
The footer is the bottom section of a page. It contains links to further content, legal information and contact details. The content usually consists of [link lists](/en/design-system/lyne/components/link-list/). In the "clock-columns" variant it is arranged in columns.

## When should the component be used?
* On every page of a website, to provide consistent information and navigation.
* To offer contact details, legal information and further links.

## Rules.
* The footer contains only relevant information, for example legal notice, privacy policy and contact.
* The footer should not be overloaded. The most important information is easy to find.
* The SBB clock is a fixed part of the footer and may only be removed for internal applications.
* The footer needs a title for screen readers (`accessibility-title`). It is not visually displayed.


## What does the component do?
An icon is a graphic symbol. It visually represents functions, content or actions.

## When should the component be used?
* To visually support and emphasise functions or actions.
* To save space.
* To make information or status quickly recognisable.
* To support navigation.

## Rules.
* Icons come in 3 sizes (24 x 24, 36 x 36 and 48 x 48 pixels). Other sizes are not permitted.
* Icons have a stroke width of 1 pixel. Exceptions are only permitted in the header.
* The size of the icon matches the surrounding text and other elements.
* Icons are used sparingly and only where they improve usability.
* An icon on its own is not understandable for screen readers and is hidden by default. Decorative icons stay hidden. If an icon conveys information or is interactive, it needs a text alternative (e.g. `aria-label` on the surrounding element) or a [tooltip](/en/design-system/lyne/components/tooltip/).

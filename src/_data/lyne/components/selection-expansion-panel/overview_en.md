
## What does the component do?
The selection expansion panel wraps a [checkbox panel](/en/design-system/lyne/components/checkbox-panel/) or a [radio button panel](/en/design-system/lyne/components/radio-button-panel/) and displays additional content for it.

## When should the component be used?
* When additional content should be displayed for an option, e.g. sub-options or information.

## Rules.
* The content opens as soon as the panel is selected. Alternatively, it is always displayed.
* Clicking the content area does not change the selection.
* Selection expansion panels are only used within a [checkbox group](/en/design-system/lyne/components/checkbox-group/) or [radio button group](/en/design-system/lyne/components/radio-button-group/).
* For an option with its own action, combine the selection expansion panel with the [selection action panel](/en/design-system/lyne/components/selection-action-panel/).

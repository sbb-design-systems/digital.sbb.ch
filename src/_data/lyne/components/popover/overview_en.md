
## What does the component do?
A popover is a small [overlay](/en/design-system/lyne/components/overlay/) that shows additional information or interactive content for an element. It opens on click or, optionally, on hover over the element.

## When should the component be used?
* To explain icons or buttons.
* To explain terms or help users make decisions without leaving the page.
* For plain, short text without interaction, use the [tooltip](/en/design-system/lyne/components/tooltip/) instead.

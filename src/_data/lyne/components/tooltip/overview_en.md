
## What does the component do?
The tooltip displays additional information about an element without interrupting the flow.

## When should the component be used?
* To explain icons, abbreviations or technical terms.
* For supplementary notes on controls.

## Rules.
* The text is short, precise and understandable.
* Use text only. No interactive elements such as buttons or links.
* Essential content and mandatory information do not belong in a tooltip.
* The tooltip opens on hover, and on touch devices by long press. Keyboard users must also be able to access the information.

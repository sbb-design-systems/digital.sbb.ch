## What does the component do?
Hides content behind a title. The corresponding content can be shown or hidden by clicking on the title.

## When should the component be used?
* To structure content and improve the information architecture of a page.
* To avoid long scrolling.

## Rules.
* An accordion consists of at least two entries.
* Accordions are not nested.
* By default, only one entry is open at a time. Several open entries are possible (`multi`).
* The titles are marked up as headings (`titleLevel`) matching the heading hierarchy of the page.

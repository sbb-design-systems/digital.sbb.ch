
## What does the component do?
An expansion panel hides content behind a title. Clicking the title expands or collapses the content.

## When should the component be used?
* To offer additional information that not all users need, e.g. details, explanations or frequently asked questions.
* To make long pages clearer by showing content only when needed.

## Rules.
* The title describes the hidden content briefly and clearly.
* Information that all users must see does not belong in a collapsed panel.
* Expansion panels are not nested.
* Several related panels are grouped in the [accordion](/en/design-system/lyne/components/accordion/).
* The title is marked up with the appropriate heading level.

## What does the component do?
An alert shows important information or warnings to users. By default, it can be closed. It can also be displayed as non-closable (readonly).

## When should the component be used?
* To inform about critical errors, warnings or important information.
* To bring immediate attention to critical messages.

## Rules.
* An alert contains a title and a clear, concise message.
* Alerts are placed in an alert group. It controls spacing and accessibility (new alerts are announced by screen readers).
* Alerts and [notifications](/en/design-system/lyne/components/notification/) are not combined.
* Alerts are not used for error messages in forms.

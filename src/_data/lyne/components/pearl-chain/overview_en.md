
## What does the component do?
The pearl chain displays a journey as a chain of points connected by lines. Each point represents a stop. It shows the progress of the journey with the current position, as well as disruptions, walking sections and uncertain sections. It can be used horizontally or vertically.

## When should the component be used?
* To display the course of a connection with its stops, e.g. in a connection overview or a travel plan.
* To show travellers where they currently are on their journey.

## Rules.
* The content of a card belongs to the same topic and is worded concisely.
* A card usually has one action. In this case, the entire card is the click target (card link or card button).
* If a card has several interactive elements, the click targets are placed on these elements.
* The text of a card link and card button is meaningful. It is only read by screen readers and search engines.
* A card badge shows additional information such as prices or discounts and needs an `aria-label` with a complete sentence.

## What does the component do?
The teaser product promotes a product with text and an optional footnote on an image. On small screens, the content follows the image. There are two variants:
* Teaser product: The whole area is clickable and leads to a target.
* Teaser product static: The area is not clickable. Only the elements it contains, for example buttons or links, are interactive.

## When should the component be used?
* To highlight a product or service with an image and lead to the product page.
* When the text may be longer than in the [simple teaser](/en/design-system/lyne/components/teaser/), e.g. with a title and footnote.
* The teaser product static suits cases where the whole area should not be clickable or where there is more than one action.


## What does the component do?
The train component displays the train formation: the order of the wagons, their class, occupancy and facilities, as well as the sectors on the platform and the direction of travel. This helps travellers find the right wagon quickly.

## When should the component be used?
* To show travellers where on the platform each wagon stops, e.g. first class, dining car or bicycle compartment.
* To display the occupancy of individual wagons.
* To make wagons selectable, e.g. for a reservation.
